# Friend walkthroughs (qualitative research, not a launch)

Use this when showing the **testnet** site to people you know. Goal: learn whether the *idea* is intelligible and whether they can see themselves (or someone they know) doing a **job** for a **cause they already care about**.

This is not user acquisition. Do not ask them to become users of Commonality. Do not apologize for the product being unfinished; frame it as "I want to know if this makes sense."

Related: [README.md](./README.md), [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md), [activating-a-cause](/docs/founder/activating-a-cause.md), [the jobs](/docs/end-user/commonality/the-jobs.md), [how-to-convey-this.md](../how-to-convey-this.md).

## Who to show

Prefer people who already *do* something: donate, organize a club, run a Discord, write, volunteer, sit on a tiny board. A purely curious friend will rate "interesting" and teach you nothing.

Skip (for now) people whose first move is always "is this a security?" unless you want that specific objection. One such conversation is enough.

5–8 sessions beat 30 unstructured demos. After three identical confusions, stop and change the product/copy.

## Setup (you, before they arrive)

- Pick **one URL**. Commonality testnet *or* Civility *or* CSM — not a tour of eight domains. (Phase 0 in [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md).)
- Pick **one story** on that URL that is fully seeded (pledges, a project, a vouch, or a bridge cluster). Narrate that story; do not browse.
- Say out loud, once: this is testnet, fake/test money, some names are synthetic, nothing here is a live fundraiser.
- Have a fallback if wallets fail: you drive, they talk. A stuck wallet invalidates the rest of the session as research.

## Script (30–40 minutes)

You talk less than they do. After the one-sentence frame, questions only.

**Frame (20 seconds):** "This is a way for people who already care about something to put money and judgment on it without founding an organization. I want to know if that sentence is nonsense once you see it."

1. **Before the screen (3 min).** "What cause or local thing have you actually given time or money to in the last year?" Write it down. Everything later maps back to *their* cause, not ours.

2. **Silent first look (2 min).** Open the URL. "Click whatever you'd click. Think out loud. I won't help yet." Note where they go and what they think the page *is* (petition, Kickstarter, charity, party, Discord).

3. **Comprehension (5 min).** "In your own words, what is this for?" If they say Kickstarter/blockchain/DAO, ask "what's different from that, if anything?" Do not correct yet.

4. **Their cause (8 min).** "If this existed for [their cause], what would you actually do?" Offer the [job menu](/docs/end-user/commonality/the-jobs.md) only if they stall: pledge and forget, scout, vouch that work succeeded, publish a project, share with people they trust. Note which jobs they reject and why.

5. **Roles for people they know (5 min).** "Who in your world would be the donor / the picker / the doer / the person with an audience?" If they cannot name anyone, the unbundling did not land.

6. **Show one mechanism (5 min), only after they have guessed.** One of: refund-if-it-fails, delegate the picking, sign your own words, or "you don't join." Stop at one. Extra mechanisms are how demos become lectures.

7. **Would they forward it? (3 min).** "Would you send *this page* to someone for [their cause], or would you explain it in a text first?" If they need a paragraph of preamble, the share artifact is missing.

8. **Close.** Thank them. Ask permission to keep anonymized quotes. Optional: "Want a note when [their kind of cause] can try this for real?" That is a waitlist of *causes*, not a newsletter.

## What to write down (same night)

Append to [notes.md](./notes.md). Per person:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Their cause | church building fund, local trails, "not the Twitter fight" |
| What they thought it was | Kickstarter for NGOs |
| Restatement | accurate / mapped-wrong / blank |
| Role they'd take | hands-off donor / none / organizer |
| Roles they'd recruit | "my uncle would pick projects" |
| Would forward the URL? | no — would text a summary |
| Blocker | wallet / empty board / "who's in charge?" / securities vibe |
| Quote worth keeping | … |

**Success for a session** is not enthusiasm. It is: they mapped it onto their cause, named at least one job, and you captured one confusion you can fix.

## Confusions we already expect

Treat these as hypotheses to confirm, not surprises:

- They look for an organization, a leader, or a "join" button ([cause-page-not-a-club](../cause-page-not-a-club.md)).
- They think signing is a petition to government.
- They think pledging is donating to *you*.
- Empty or synthetic-looking boards feel like a scam.
- Wallet / "crypto" ends the conversation before the idea starts.
- They want *their* people and *their* URL, not Commonality.

If a new confusion shows up twice, add it to [notes.md](./notes.md) and to landing/docs — do not add a feature first.

## What not to do

- Do not send a group chat "check out my startup." One-on-one, scheduled, with their cause in mind.
- Do not demo every subsystem. Implication graphs and secondary-market history are founder-only in this setting.
- Do not use mainnet or real money.
- Do not "correct" their restatement until after you've written it down. Their wrong mapping *is* the data.
- Do not count "this is cool" as evidence. Cool is the default polite response to a friend.
