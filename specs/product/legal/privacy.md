# Privacy

Trust graphs plus emails plus on-chain activity is personal information under PIPEDA; a counsel-reviewed privacy policy and data-handling story are table stakes.

The `PublishedData` migration reduces Commonality's role as the primary host of user-authored text, but it does not make privacy risk disappear. User publications and social/funding activity are permanently recoverable from public chain history, retraction is display suppression rather than erasure, and Commonality designs and operates the initial readers. Statements of belief may reveal sensitive or special-category information. Before mainnet, document data flows and retention for the UI/API/indexer, avoid unnecessary address-to-identity linkage, obtain meaningful permanence consent, expose retraction, and get Canadian counsel plus GDPR advice if EU users are expected. See [statement-hosting.md](statement-hosting.md#permanence-cuts-back-the-users-side-of-the-bargain).

See also [privacy-slider.md](/specs/product/privacy-slider.md) for the product-side thinking about how much a user reveals.
