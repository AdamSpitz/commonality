# Provide trust or reach without owning the work

Maybe what you have is credibility in a community, knowledge of who is reliable,
or an audience that pays attention. That connective capacity is useful on its own.
You do not have to turn it into an organization that receives and spends every
dollar.

## You do not also have to

- **Fund the project.** Vouching can put it in front of people and delegates who do
  have capital.
- **Do the project.** An alignment vouch says the project genuinely relates to a
  statement; it does not make you its operator.
- **Guarantee success.** Alignment and success are separate judgments. Vouch only
  for the claim you can support.
- **Allocate anyone’s money.** Recommend a trusted scout or delegate without
  becoming one yourself.
- **Make everyone agree.** Share the relevant statement or cause board. People can
  sign and support individual statements without accepting the whole mix.

## How your trust or reach recruits the other roles

A vouch moves a project from an isolated link into the fundable-projects boards of
people whose trust networks reach you. That distribution helps scouts find it and
helps builders reach capital without cold-pitching everyone. Recommending a good
delegate lets donors participate without acquiring domain expertise. Sharing
delivered work attracts evaluators and retrospective funders; sharing visible
pledges signals demand to potential builders.

Trust and distribution are not decorative marketing after the “real work.” They
are the bridge that lets money, judgment, and execution find one another.

## Start here

Vouch only where you understand the relationship: that a project aligns with a
statement, or that it delivered. Circulate the cause-board or project link to the
people for whom it is relevant. If nearby communities use different wording, help
connect them with [suggestions and bridges](../../tally/suggestions-and-nudges.md).
