# Do useful work without becoming a fundraiser

If you can execute a concrete piece of work, propose that work as a project. You do
not need to incorporate an organization that also owns the donor list, evaluates
grants, publishes a manifesto, and manages every future project for the cause.

## You do not also have to

- **Pay for the work yourself.** Set the amount required; contributors pledge
  toward the threshold.
- **Bring a complete donor network.** Get a trusted alignment vouch onto a statement
  people already watch. The project can then appear on their cause boards.
- **Convince every donor personally.** Scouts and delegates can evaluate the
  proposal on behalf of people who prefer hands-off giving.
- **Carry the risk of a half-funded attempt.** If the assurance threshold is not
  reached, contributors are refunded and the project does not pretend the budget
  was sufficient.
- **Organize the whole cause.** Deliver one bounded project. Other people can
  publish the cause board, maintain trust, fund, and evaluate results.

## How your work recruits the other roles

A concrete project turns broad agreement into an actionable funding target. That
gives donors a reason to pledge, scouts something they can recommend, and
connectors something specific to distribute. Delivering creates evidence for
evaluators. Their success vouches can attract retrospective donors, whose money
replenishes early contributors and makes them able to scout the next builder.

Execution is one spoke of the flywheel, not the institution that must manufacture
all the other spokes.

## Start here

Use **Start project** from a cause board, or open `/projects/new`. Describe the
deliverable, amount, and deadline, then ask someone trusted by the relevant
community to vouch that the project aligns with one of its statements. The fuller
walkthrough is **[Get your project funded](../../lazyGiving/get-your-project-funded.md)**.
