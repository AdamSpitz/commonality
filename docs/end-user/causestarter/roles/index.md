# Choose the part you can provide

A cause usually needs money, judgment, skilled work, trust, distribution, and
someone willing to keep the invitation visible. CauseStarter does **not** require
one person or organization to provide all of them.

Pick the capacity you actually have. Each guide says what the role contributes,
what you can leave to other people, and how doing your part makes those other
people easier to recruit.

- **[I can provide money](./fund.md)** — from a modest pledge to a whale-sized
  commitment, without also becoming a grant officer, fundraiser, or operator.
- **[I can spot promising work](./scout.md)** — use subject knowledge and
  prospective judgment, even if you cannot personally supply much capital.
- **[I can recognize work that delivered](./evaluate.md)** — inspect results,
  vouch for success, or fund proven work without betting on pitches.
- **[I can do the work](./build.md)** — propose and execute a concrete project
  without also building a donor network or charitable organization.
- **[I have trust or an audience](./connect.md)** — help good work travel through
  vouches, delegation, and distribution without taking responsibility for doing
  or funding it yourself.
- **[I can organize a cause](./organize.md)** — publish the shared invitation and
  recruit each capacity separately instead of staffing an all-purpose institution.

These are not memberships or permanent identities. Take one role on one project,
several roles when you want to, or stop after a single useful contribution.

For the obstacle-by-obstacle version, see **[Do the part you’d do
anyway](../the-jobs.md)**. If you are assembling the invitation, see **[Start a
cause](../start-a-cause.md)**.
