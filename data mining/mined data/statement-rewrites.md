# Mined statements → CauseStarter drafts

Source: [`causestarter-statement-examples.md`](./causestarter-statement-examples.md) (verbatim rows collected 2026-08-25; Churches of Christ ceasefire rows #8 and #28 removed).

This pass does **not** load the raw quotes. It rewrites them against the repo statement bar in `cause-assist/src/statementGuidance.ts` and `fake-data-generation/statement-generation.md`: a statement is a plain-English proposition a person would sign; one claim; determinate; self-contained; want the outcome, do not classify it; prefer 1–2 sentences; drop slogans, questionnaire stems, ALL CAPS, and party captions. Place and number stay when they were the grain of the original.

Loaded into Chrome CauseStarter (`http://localhost:8090/`) as local drafts (`causestarter.causes.v3`). Not published on-chain.

## Counts

- Original rows: 108 (110 minus Churches of Christ #8 and #28)
- Improved planks: 116 (some originals split)
- Cause boards: 22

## What changed, in bulk

| Pattern | What we did |
|---|---|
| Questionnaire stems (Pew, WVS) | Turned into the proposition the survey is used to measure. |
| Kialo / pamphlet questions | Turned the thesis into a signable claim (con poles kept as their own planks). |
| ALL CAPS platform slogans | Sentence case, one want, tribe-markers like “invasion” dropped when they were slogans not the policy. |
| Stacked agenda sentences | Split into independent planks. |
| “As Democrats” / “Republicans will” | Dropped the team caption; kept the civic claim. |
| Mission / vision / “pillar in the coordination engine” | Outcome want, not taxonomy. |
| “I hope” / “we will” Polis lines | “I want …”, with Bowling Green or other place left on. |
| German Klimarat | English rendering of the same claim. |
| Town-meeting and bond legalese | The civic want, with the named place and number. |
