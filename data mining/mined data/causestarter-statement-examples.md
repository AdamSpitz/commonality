Collected 2026-08-25 (America/New_York). Verbatim quotes only. Demographics are inferred from the publishing community, or copied from a cited survey breakout. Numeric splits appear only when a cited report states them.

## Product frame used

CauseStarter publishes a versioned CAUSE as independently signable PLANKS. Rows are typed as `cause` (title/summary), `goal`, `belief`, or `plank` (one discrete claim a person could sign). Excluded: Change.org-style slogans as the main diet, marketing taglines, multi-clause rants, invented copy, sister-app or private CauseStarter text.
